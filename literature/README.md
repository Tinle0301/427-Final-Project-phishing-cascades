# Literature

Put paper notes in `notes.md` — one entry per paper, with the takeaway in one
line. The rubric grades the quality of the literature review, and the proposed
solution has to be grounded in it.

## Where to start

- **Network epidemiology** — Pastor-Satorras & Vespignani on epidemic spreading
  in scale-free networks; the idea that a highly skewed degree distribution
  removes the epidemic threshold
- **Targeted immunization** — work comparing random vs degree-targeted vs
  acquaintance immunization
- **Influence maximization** — Kempe, Kleinberg & Tardos, for picking the k most
  influential nodes
- **Phishing susceptibility** — empirical studies of who clicks and why
- **The dataset itself** — the paper the email-Eu-core network came from

Use Google Scholar and the CSULB library. Open the papers you cite; do not cite
from an abstract.
